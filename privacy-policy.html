1. Introduction

SnapToSolve ("we", "us", "our") is an AI-powered homework solving application operated by Girish Shankaran. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal data.

This policy complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) for users in India and includes additional rights for users in the United States (§14), United Kingdom (§15), and European Union (§16).

By using SnapToSolve, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Definitions

  • "Personal Data" means any data about a natural person which is directly or indirectly identifiable.
  • "Child" means any person under 18 years of age (India/EU/UK) or under 13 (US/COPPA).
  • "Data Fiduciary / Controller" means the entity that determines the purpose and means of processing personal data (Girish Shankaran).
  • "Data Processor" means any person or service that processes personal data on our behalf (e.g., Google, Razorpay, Stripe, Render).

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Consent: You provide consent at first launch via the Privacy Consent screen. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.
  • Contractual necessity: Processing required to deliver the subscription service you purchase.
  • Legitimate interests: Security, fraud prevention, abuse detection (e.g., preventing free-trial abuse after account deletion).
  • Legal obligation: Compliance with applicable laws (tax records, regulatory requests).

5. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide and improve AI homework solutions
  • Manage your subscription, quota, and payment history
  • Send push notifications (quota alerts, reminders) — only with permission
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and policy violations
  • Analyse aggregated usage patterns to improve the product
  • Respond to support queries and grievances
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for their marketing purposes.

8. Account Deletion & Data Erasure

You can delete your account at any time from: App → Profile → Delete Account.

Upon deletion:

  • Your email, name, and profile information are immediately anonymised
  • All homework images and solutions are deleted within 30 days
  • Your subscription flags are reset
  • A hashed fraud-prevention flag may be retained to prevent free-trial abuse (contains no personal data)
  • Payment transaction records are retained for 7 years as required by law

To request deletion of payment records beyond what in-app deletion covers, email us at snapsolve.edupp@gmail.com.

9. Security

  • All data transmitted between the app and server uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Passwords are never stored — authentication is via Google OAuth 2.0
  • Database access is restricted to authenticated backend services only
  • Payment data is handled entirely by Razorpay/Stripe — we never see raw card data
  • Admin access to the backend is protected by a secret key

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no system is 100% secure. In the event of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

SnapToSolve is primarily a homework-solving tool used by students, including minors. Under the DPDP Act 2023:

  • Users under 18 require verifiable parental consent before using the app
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (US) or 18 (India) without parental consent
  • We do not serve behavioural advertising to minors
  • Parents/guardians may request deletion of their child's data by emailing us
If you are a parent and believe your child under 13 has provided data without consent, contact us immediately at snapsolve.edupp@gmail.com and we will delete the account within 48 hours.
